GeoJSON and Shapefile geometry validator

Check geometry validity and spatial relationships in your 2D GeoJSON or Shapefile data, then download traceable CSV or JSON issue reports. Files are processed locally in your browser.

1. Choose a file

One UTF-8 .geojson/.json, .shp, or single-layer Shapefile .zip. Processing runs locally in a browser Worker.
